Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Fari is a free menstrual cycle tracker for Android published by Mohammed Matheen ("we"). This policy describes exactly what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • Fari has no servers, no advertising and no analytics.
  • You sign in with your Google account so that only you can open your data. Fari keeps your Google account's name and email address on your phone and does not send them to us.
  • Everything you enter stays on your phone, encrypted. We never receive it and cannot see it.
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you export, back up or share it yourself.

Google sign-in

Fari asks you to sign in with Google. The sign-in is performed by Google Play services on your phone under Google's Privacy Policy. Fari receives your Google account's unique identifier, email address and name, and stores them in its encrypted database on your phone to recognise the account that owns the data. Fari requests only the basic openid, email and profile permissions. It does not request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and does not send your account details or cycle records to us or anyone else.

What Fari stores on your device

  • Information you choose to enter: an optional display name, period dates, daily flow, symptoms and their severity, moods, notes, your typical cycle and period length, and an optional manual estimate.
  • App settings: reminder choices and times, quiet hours, notification wording, theme, app lock and privacy-screen preferences.
  • Calculated estimates of upcoming periods, derived from your own records.
  • The Google account details described above.

This information is stored in an encrypted database on your device. The encryption key is generated on your device and protected by Android's hardware-backed Keystore.

What Fari does not do

  • The app itself has no internet permission, so it cannot send your data anywhere. (Google sign-in is handled by Google Play services.)
  • It does not collect device identifiers, advertising IDs, location, contacts or usage analytics.
  • It does not include advertising or tracking software.
  • It does not sell, rent or share personal or health information with anyone.
  • It does not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.

Notifications

Reminders are generated and scheduled on your device. By default they use discreet wording, and the lock screen shows only the discreet version unless you choose otherwise.

Exports, backups and sharing (only when you choose)

  • CSV and PDF exports are saved to a location you pick with Android's file picker. They are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.
  • Encrypted backups are protected with a passphrase you choose (PBKDF2-SHA256 and AES-256-GCM). We cannot recover a backup if the passphrase is lost.
  • Sharing a message opens Android's share sheet with text you can see and edit first. The app you choose to share with handles that message under its own privacy policy.

Android backup

Fari opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your records are not copied to Google or another phone automatically. Use Fari's encrypted backup to move to a new phone.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Settings › Privacy & data › Delete account and all data. This permanently erases everything Fari stores, including the linked Google account details, destroys the encryption key and signs you out. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of its data.

Because Fari has no server, there is no account or data held anywhere else to delete. Copies you exported earlier are outside the app and are not affected. You can also remove Fari's access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections. For help, email mohdmatheen@gmail.com.

Children

Fari is not directed to children under 13.

Health information

Fari provides period tracking and general wellness information. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose any condition and is not a method of contraception.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date. Any future optional feature that sends data off the device will be off by default, explained before use, and reflected here first.
